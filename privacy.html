Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026. Applies to RoleRadar for Mac, its Safari extension, and the RoleRadar Apify plugin.

The short version: RoleRadar doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your résumé, cover letters, roles and notes stay on your Mac, and fit reads run on your Mac. The developer of RoleRadar receives none of it. RoleRadar only contacts the job sources and services described below, so it can find roles for you.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your résumé, cover letters, drafts, roles, notes and decisions are stored in RoleRadar's own storage on your Mac.
  • Fit reads and wording suggestions run on your Mac, using Apple's on-device model or, if you choose one in Settings, an open model downloaded to your Mac. Your résumé is never sent to an AI service.
  • Your settings (search terms, places, companies, sources) are stored on your Mac.
  • Your name and contact details, your résumé form and the skills you add (My Skills) are stored on your Mac and used only there, for fit reads, templates and file names.
  • Notifications are created on your Mac. RoleRadar doesn't use push notification servers.

What goes out, and to whom

To find roles, RoleRadar makes ordinary web requests from your Mac's internet connection. The services it contacts see that request, including your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply.

Your location

If you click Current Location in Settings ▸ Location, macOS asks for your permission and gives RoleRadar one approximate position. RoleRadar matches it to the nearest town on your Mac and saves only that town as a search place. The position itself isn't stored or sent anywhere. You can always type a town instead.

The Safari extension

The RoleRadar Safari extension reads the page you're on only when you click its button, so it can check whether the page is a job posting. If you add it, the posting is handed to RoleRadar on your Mac. The extension's one internet request: when a company's page shows a job from its Greenhouse job board, it asks Greenhouse's public job board for that posting (only the job's number is sent). It sends nothing else.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with the developer. You control that setting; RoleRadar has no analytics of its own.

Plugins and RoleRadar Apify

Plugins are separate apps that add job sources needing an account. RoleRadar uses a plugin only after you turn it on in Settings ▸ Plugins. When it does, RoleRadar gives the plugin your search terms, places, remote preference, experience level, excluded companies and search filters. It never gives a plugin your résumé, cover letters, notes or the skills you add.

RoleRadar Apify, available separately from roleradar.app, runs the searches you choose on Apify using your own Apify account:

  • It sends Apify your search terms, places, the settings of the Apify actors you choose (for LinkedIn, also any title words you choose to skip), and your experience level when you choose to skip junior titles. Results come back to your Mac.
  • Your Apify API token is stored in the plugin's own keychain on your Mac and is sent only to Apify. RoleRadar itself never sees it.
  • Runs happen on your Apify account, under Apify's privacy policy and terms, and any costs are charged by Apify to that account. You're responsible for using the actors you choose in line with the terms of the sites they search.

Macs managed by an organization

An employer, school or career service can set some of RoleRadar's settings with a configuration profile (for example, companies to block or sources to use). RoleRadar only reads those settings. It sends nothing back to the organization: your roles, résumé and notes stay on your Mac.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because everything is on your Mac, you're in control of it:

  • Moving roles to the Trash and emptying it erases them.
  • Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ Reset RoleRadar… erases your roles, drafts and settings.
  • Deleting RoleRadar removes its storage from your Mac. Documents you exported, and sign-ins kept by websites, stay where they are until you delete them.

The developer holds no copy of your data, so there's nothing to request or delete on our side.

Children

RoleRadar is a job-search tool for adults and isn't directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. RoleRadar won't start collecting your data without saying so here first.
