Support

Help is built into the app: choose Help ▸ RoleRadar Help.

Contact

Email support@roleradar.app. It helps to include your macOS version and what you were doing. For a sweep problem, set Settings ▸ General ▸ Activity log to Detailed, try again, and include what Activity ▸ Log shows.

Common questions

How do I restore RoleRadar Pro on another Mac?

Choose RoleRadar ▸ Upgrade to RoleRadar Pro… or open Settings ▸ General, and click Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Why didn't a sweep find anything?

Check your search terms and places in Settings, and look in Activity: each source says what it found, or why it didn't run.

Why can't I sign in with a passkey in RoleRadar's browser?

macOS lets only full web browsers use passkeys. Sign in with your password instead, or click Open in Safari.

How do I remove all my data?

Settings ▸ General ▸ Reset RoleRadar… erases your roles, drafts and settings. See the privacy policy.
