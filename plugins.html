Plugins

Plugins add job sources that need an account. Each is a small separate app that RoleRadar uses only after you turn it on.

RoleRadar Apify

Job sources that run on your own Apify account. Choose Apify actors to search for you; results arrive in your sweeps. Runs and any costs are on your Apify account, and your Apify token stays in the plugin.

Version 1.0 · Requires RoleRadar Pro for its sources to run in sweeps.

Installing a plugin

  1. Download the plugin and move it to your Applications folder.
  2. Open it once, and set it up (for RoleRadar Apify, connect your Apify token).
  3. In RoleRadar, open Settings ▸ Plugins and click Turn On…, then turn the plugin on in the panel.

To remove a plugin, click Show in Finder next to it in Settings ▸ Plugins and move it to the Trash.

For organizations

Deploy a plugin's installer with your MDM like any Mac app, and control which plugins RoleRadar uses with the PluginsAllowed and AllowedPlugins keys. See RoleRadar's MDM guide.
